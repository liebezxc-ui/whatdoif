Whatdoif Publisher

A personal desktop tool of the "whatdoif" channel: short animated series «Что делать, если…» about ordinary people in extraordinary situations.

The app runs on the channel author's computer. When the author presses the publish button, it uploads his own finished video and caption to his own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and VK accounts through their official APIs. It has a single user and no server.
