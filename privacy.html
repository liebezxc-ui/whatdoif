Whatdoif Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Whatdoif Publisher ("the app") is a personal desktop tool. Its only user is the author of the "whatdoif" channel, and it runs only on his computer.

1. Data the app receives. When the author signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, username) for the author's own account. It receives similar access tokens from YouTube, Instagram and VK when those accounts are connected.

2. How it is used. Tokens are used only to upload the author's own videos and captions to his own accounts when he presses the publish button, and to show which account is connected.

3. Storage. Tokens and settings are stored in a local file on the author's computer. They are not sent to any server other than the official API of the platform they belong to.

4. Sharing. The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no advertising and no backend server.

5. Other people's data. The app does not access followers, comments, messages or any data of other users.

6. Deletion. Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens from the computer. Access can also be revoked in the platform's account settings.

7. Changes. This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
